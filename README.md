# Daniel Rezinovsky

**Artist · psychologist · researcher**

I work across consciousness, artificial intelligence, immersive media and experimental systems.

My projects use digital environments, symbolic interfaces and interactive technologies to explore altered perception, encounter, unusual forms of mind, and the changing relationship between consciousness and technology.

## Selected work

### Way
A light-runner and audiovisual journey through seven worlds of speed, geometry and music. Currently in active development.

### [Ontik](https://github.com/danrezi-gif/Ontik-vr-shader-experience)
Immersive WebXR worlds of light, motion and sound exploring unfamiliar modes of perception.

### [New Kinds of Minds](https://github.com/danrezi-gif/new-kinds-of-minds)
A participatory global atlas of neurodiversity initiatives, communities, research groups and alternative institutions.

### [Quantum I Ching](https://github.com/danrezi-gif/quantum-iching)
An interactive I Ching oracle bringing hardware quantum randomness into dialogue with AI interpretation.

### [Mindlamp / Quantum Sphere](https://github.com/danrezi-gif/Quantum-Sphere)
An audiovisual experiment around attention, intention and quantum randomness.

### [Sangha](https://github.com/danrezi-gif/sangha)
A swarm artwork in which artificial agents associated with contemplative traditions meet, form affinities and generate emergent conversations.

## Developing work

[Encontro](https://github.com/danrezi-gif/encontro) explores encounter through luminous WebXR presences. [Mu](https://github.com/danrezi-gif/mu-chat) explores a quieter, less instrumental form of conversation with AI.
